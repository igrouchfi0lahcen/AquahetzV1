Our Story

Morocco faces one of the most severe water crises in the world. Every year, millions of liters are lost to hidden leaks in aging pipes — beneath farms, factories, and cities. Most of these leaks are invisible until the damage is already done.

Aquahertz ai was born from a simple idea: what if pipes could talk? Using acoustic vibration sensing and machine learning, we listen to the subtle sounds of water flow and detect anomalies before they become catastrophic leaks.

Our first prototype was tested on a small farm outside Marrakech. Today, we're building the next generation of smart water infrastructure for industrial facilities, agricultural operations, and municipalities across Morocco.
